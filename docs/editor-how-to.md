# How to edit the Belmore Estates website

Your work saves itself as a draft. Visitors see nothing until you click **Publish**.

## 1. Sign in
1. Go to the website address followed by **/login**.
2. Click **Continue with Google** and choose your Gmail account.
3. If Google doesn't work, click **Sign in with email and password instead** and use the password an owner set for you.

## 2. Start editing
1. Open any page on the site.
2. Click **✎ Edit this page** in the bottom corner.
3. To switch pages, use **Editing page** in the toolbar.

## 3. Change text and photos
1. Click any words and type over them. Press **Enter** or click elsewhere when done.
2. Click any photo to replace it, then do one of these:
   - drag in a new photo
   - pick an earlier upload (**Your photos**)
3. Fill in **Describe the photo** (it helps Google and visitors who can't see images), then click **Use this photo**.
4. Photos must be JPG, PNG or WebP under 8 MB. They're resized automatically, and location data is removed.

## 4. Work with sections
1. Click the edge of a section to select it. A small bar appears with these buttons:
   - **↑ ↓** move it
   - **Hide** keeps it for later without showing it to visitors
   - the copy icon duplicates it
   - **🗑** deletes it after asking you to confirm
2. The right-hand panel changes its background and spacing.
3. To add something, click **Add a section** on the left and drag a block (for example Questions & answers, Video or Google map) onto the page.
4. Deleted an original section by mistake? Drag it back from **Add a section → Original sections**.

## 5. Add a page and a menu link
1. Click **Pages & menu** on the left, then **+ New page**.
2. Type the page name. The web address fills itself in.
3. Add a Google title and description if you like.
4. Leave **Add a link to the menu** ticked and click **Create page**.
5. Under **Menu** you can rename, reorder (↑ ↓), hide (◉) or remove links, and **+ Add a menu link** to a page or to another website.

## 6. Change colors and fonts
1. Click **Colors & fonts**.
2. Pick a ready-made color set, or click a color (for example **Main color**) and choose a new one.
3. Choose heading and body fonts, the button shape and the spacing.
4. Change the logo to text or an image.
5. **Reset to the original look** brings back the Belmore design.

## 7. Preview
1. **Preview** opens your draft in a new tab, as visitors would see it.
2. **Desktop / Tablet / Mobile** in the toolbar checks smaller screens.

## 8. Publish
1. Click **Publish…**.
2. Optionally type a note (for example "Spring prices"), then click **Yes, publish to the live site**.
3. Visitors see it right away. Every published version is kept.

## 9. Undo
1. Click **↶ Undo** (or press Ctrl+Z) to step back, and **↷ Redo** to step forward again.
2. Undo covers everything: text, photos, sections, pages, menu and colors.

## 10. Restore an earlier version
1. Click **Versions** on the left.
2. Find the version you want. **Original site** is the design before the editor.
3. Click **Restore** and confirm. This replaces your draft only.
4. Check it, then **Publish** to make it live. Changed your mind? Click **Undo**.

**Tip:** If you see "changed in another window", close the other editor tab and reload.
